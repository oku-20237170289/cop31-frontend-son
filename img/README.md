# Images

Put photos here with exactly these names. Any that are missing stay hidden on the
published site; on Live Server you'll see a labelled box where each one goes.

Size: landscape 3:2, about 1500 × 1000 px, JPEG (or WebP renamed to match), under ~200 KB each.
Other shapes still work; they're cropped to fill the 3:2 frame.
After adding a photo, edit its `alt_…` (what it shows) and `cap_…` (caption + credit)
entries in `js/i18n.js`, in both languages.

| File | Page → section | Suggested subject |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| about-cop.jpg | About → What COP31 is | a plenary at a previous COP, or the Antalya EXPO Center |
| about-team.jpg | About → Who is behind it | IDD ORG at a COP / UN event |
| culture-antalya.jpg | Culture → Antalya in November | Kaleiçi old town or the coast |
| culture-sustain.jpg | Culture → Travelling sustainably | local produce at a market |
| powered-by-google-translate.png | next to every Google translation | Google's official badge (see below) |

**Licences.** Only use photos you're allowed to publish, and credit them in the caption.
For COP photos, look at the COP31 Media Library (cop31.tr/media-library) and the UNFCCC's
image library, and check each image's licence terms. IDD ORG's own photos are the simplest.

**Google badge.** Google's terms ask for its "Powered by Google Translate" graphic next to
translations. Download the badge zip from docs.cloud.google.com/translate/attribution, pick
one, and save it here as `powered-by-google-translate.png`.

The `icon-*.png` files are the app icons, made from the IDD logo. Don't rename them.
